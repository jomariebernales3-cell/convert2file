# Convert2File app guide

Convert2File is a local document and study-material app.

- Home is the dashboard. It contains the Offline AI Assistant, recent materials, progress, and a button to select a readable recent material as AI context.
- The Offline AI Assistant works after a GGUF model is imported in Settings > Offline AI. It can answer general questions without any selected material. Selecting a recent TXT, DOCX, PDF, PPT, or PPTX file gives the assistant additional context.
- Settings > Offline AI opens the Llama Model manager. Import a `.gguf` model there, then the app loads it locally on Android. Local answers can be slower because the phone runs the model.
- To convert a file, use the conversion or upload flow, choose the output type and options, then generate and open, share, or download the result from preview or History.
- History keeps created materials and outputs. Study Builder creates and edits study sets and can use Offline AI to generate or improve draft questions.
